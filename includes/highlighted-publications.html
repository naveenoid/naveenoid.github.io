 PolyTouch: A Robust Multi-Modal Tactile Sensor for Contact-rich Manipulation
J. Zhao, Naveen Kuppuswamy, S. Feng, B. Burchfiel, E. Adelson
ICRA 2025 🏆 Best Paper Award, Field and Service Robotics
A durable, easy-to-manufacture robot finger integrating camera-based tactile sensing, acoustic sensing, and peripheral vision into a unified design with 20× longer lifespan than commercial sensors. Paired with tactile-diffusion policies, PolyTouch demonstrates that multi-modal haptic feedback significantly outperforms vision-only approaches in contact-rich bimanual tasks including egg serving, fruit sorting, and wrench insertion—particularly excelling at texture-based discrimination tasks like distinguishing blackberries from blueberries.

Ford, R. Tedrake
arXiv 2025
A systematic evaluation of Large Behavior Models through 1,800+ blind, randomized real-world trials across simulation and physical experiments. This work validates that multitask pretraining on diverse robot datasets produces policies that are more successful, more robust to distribution shifts, and dramatically more sample-efficient—enabling new complex tasks to be learned with a fraction of the training data compared to single-task baselines. Performance scales predictably with pretraining data diversity and scale.

Adaptive Compliance Policy: Learning Approximate Compliance for Diffusion Guided Control
Y. Hou, Z. Liu, C. Chi, E. Cousineau, N. Kuppuswamy, S. Feng, B. Burchfiel, S. Song
ICRA 2025
A policy framework that dynamically adjusts robot stiffness based on human demonstrations. By utilizing diffusion-guided control, the robot exhibits spatial and temporal compliance tailored to task phases, improving safety in handovers and assembly.
