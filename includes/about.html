About Me

I am passionate about building robots that meaningfully enhance human quality of life. My work is motivated by the belief that tactile sensing and haptic perception are essential for enabling robots to operate safely and effectively in unstructured, real-world environments. I focus on developing learning-based models that incorporate these signals to enable more robust and adaptable behavior.

Over the past decade, I have worked across whole-body tactile sensing, soft robotics, policy learning and Large Behavior Models (LBMs). My current research focuses on the development of haptic-informed multimodal LBMs for humanoids. My key hardware contributions include the Punyo soft bubble gripper and the PolyTouch sensor.

I hold a PhD in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Zürich. My work spans 50+ publications (1200+ citations), numerous patents, and several awards.
